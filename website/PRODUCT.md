# AudiobookPrep

## Register

brand

## Users

Independent narrators and audiobook producers preparing a manuscript before recording.

## Product Purpose

Reposition AudiobookPrep as a subscription technology product. Explain the planned AI preparation workflow and help visitors compare memberships. The current deliverable is the public website only; the future software will live at /studio/ on the same domain.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, personable. Recording-studio character with polished technology, as selected by the owner.

## Anti-references

Avoid a service-agency sales funnel, fake performance claims, synthetic testimonials, neon AI imagery, and generic feature-card repetition. The owner requested a professional technology business rather than a service business.

## Design Principles

- Put the narrator's performance first.
- Make preparation tangible through a fictional annotated manuscript.
- Explain allowances in words, with transparent example-book equivalents.
- Distinguish planned functionality from available functionality.
- Make the future studio feel like part of the same website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, adequate contrast, reduced motion, responsive text and touch targets.
