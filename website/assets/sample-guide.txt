MANUSCRIPT EXCERPT: CHAPTER 4, THE THINGS WE LEAVE UNSAID

Mara stopped at the edge of the quay. The lighthouse had been dark for seven
years. Tonight, someone had lit it.

“You came back,” Elias said. It was not a question.

She turned the brass astrolabe over in her hand, searching for an answer in
its familiar weight.
