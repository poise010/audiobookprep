A few things
worth knowing.

Good preparation starts
with clear expectations.

Can I use AudiobookPrep Studio today?+

Not yet. The new AI platform is in development. This website introduces the planned experience, with fictional sample content you can explore. Uploads, accounts, and paid memberships are not available.

Will it narrate my audiobook?+

No. The planned platform focuses on preparation: character context, chapter briefs, and pronunciation research. Your voice and performance remain yours.

Why price by words instead of books?+

A short novella and a long novel need very different amounts of preparation. Word allowances make the capacity of each plan easier to compare. Our book examples use 80,000 words per manuscript.

How should I use AI-generated prep notes?+

As a starting point, with your manuscript as the source of truth. AI can miss context or make mistakes. Verify character details and pronunciation suggestions before recording, and apply your own creative judgment.

What about private or unpublished manuscripts?+

This preview does not accept or process manuscripts. Before uploads launch, we will publish the platform’s data-handling, retention, and provider policies so you can check them against your permissions and agreements.

Can I buy a membership now?+

Not yet. Membership pricing is a proposal to help you compare the planned options. No card details are requested and no purchases can be made on this website.
