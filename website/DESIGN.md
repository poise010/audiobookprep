# AudiobookPrep design system

## Scene and direction

A narrator at a daylight-lit desk, manuscript beside the microphone, comparing a professional tool before a recording session. A white page provides the clarity of a script; a deep ink product panel recalls the recording booth.

Reference direction: the tactile controls and carefully composed product presentations of Teenage Engineering, translated into a calm narrator workspace rather than copied hardware styling. The manuscript is the brand's physical object.

## Color strategy

Restrained white architecture, deep ink sections, indigo annotation accents. Indigo at hue 280 is the Impeccable seed, deliberately confined to annotations and selected controls; no purple gradients. Pale amber is reserved for pronunciation notes. All authored CSS colors use OKLCH.

## Typography

Manrope variable, locally hosted under SIL OFL. Its open counters and softly squared construction bridge technical precision and a personable voice. Georgia appears only inside the fictional manuscript, separating story from interface. No external font requests.

## Composition

Asymmetric hero with a custom annotated manuscript scene. A slim workflow strip follows. The product section pairs a vertical feature selector with a large example guide, followed by a dark narrator-centered statement, pricing comparison, calculator and FAQ. The only repeated cards are genuinely comparable plans.

## Interaction and motion

Native details/summary FAQ, accessible button selector for illustrative guide sections, segmented billing controls, live pricing estimate, native plan dialog, responsive navigation. Press feedback 140ms; visual entry only on hero art, 500ms, reduced-motion alternative. Keyboard-initiated changes are immediate.

## Tokens

White background; ink oklch(0.21 0.017 280); primary oklch(0.47 0.16 280); muted oklch(0.47 0.02 280). Radii: 6px controls, 12px panels, 18px large product scene. Body max 70ch. Content width 1240px.
